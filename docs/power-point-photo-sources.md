style from premium designer finishes to everyday white fittings. It is not a
claim about sales rankings. Product-page links are stored alongside each card
in `src/lib/powerPointStyles.ts`. Availability and complete installed pricing
are confirmed when quoting. Iconic Styl photos show decorative covers.

## Colour audit — 12 October 2026

Searched each of the nine displayed products/ranges. Manufacturer colour selectors: Saturn Zen Z4025 = Zen White/Zen Black; Iconic Styl S3025C = Crowne/Silver Shadow/Silver; Iconic 3025C = VW/XW/AN/CY/XB plus discontinued WY; Iconic 3025USBC = VW only; 2025 = WE/BK/DB/GR/RD plus discontinued CM; C2025 = WE/BK/DB/GR/RD plus discontinued CM/BA (C2025C-BA cover separately listed). Hager silhouette gallery = gloss white/matt white/matt black/aluminium/stainless steel.

HPM Linea catalogue: https://www.hpm.com.au/sites/g/files/ocwmcr1501/files/2025-05/Linea%20Catalogue.pdf ; additional older colours documented in https://hpm.com.au/app/uploads/2018/05/hpm-linea-switches-and-sockets-brochure-2015-lr.pdf .
